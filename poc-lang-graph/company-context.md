# Company: AcmeFlow

## Overview
**AcmeFlow** is a B2B SaaS platform that provides real-time logistics, fleet tracking, and supply chain automation for medium to large enterprises. We operate primarily in North America and Europe.

## Target Audience
Our users are typically Supply Chain Managers, Fleet Dispatchers, and IT Administrators who rely on our platform to ensure goods move efficiently. 

## Our Core Products
1. **AcmeTrack:** Real-time GPS and IoT fleet tracking dashboard.
2. **AcmeRoute:** AI-driven route optimization and delivery scheduling.
3. **AcmeSync:** API gateway for ERP and Warehouse Management System integrations.

## Global Communication Policy
- **Tone:** Professional, direct, yet empathetic. We are partners, not just vendors.
- **Clarity over Jargon:** Explain technical issues simply. Avoid hiding behind complex acronyms.
- **Ownership:** Never say "it's a third-party issue" without offering a path forward. Always take ownership of the customer's success.
- **Transparency:** If we have a system outage, we say so clearly. We do not hide bugs.
