# INSTRUCTIONS
Prepare a clear investor update that summarizes progress, traction, product milestones, customer signals, hiring, financial runway, risks, and asks. Use a direct and credible tone. Avoid hype and unsupported claims.

# INPUTS
- Reporting period
- Product and business milestones
- Traction metrics
- Customer or market signals
- Financial or runway notes
- Current asks or support needed

# EXPECTED OUTPUTS
- Draft investor update
- Metrics summary
- Progress highlights
- Risks and challenges
- Specific investor asks
