# INSTRUCTIONS
Analyze a legal agreement to identify risks and ensure business protection.

1. **Deep Reading:** Read the provided contract or document thoroughly.
2. **Risk Identification:** Flag any clauses related to liability, termination, intellectual property, or data privacy that seem unfavorable.
3. **Compliance Check:** Ensure the agreement follows relevant regulations (GDPR, Tax, etc.).
4. **Refining:** Suggest specific language changes to mitigate identified risks while maintaining the business intent.

# INPUTS
- Link to the contract or full text.

# EXPECTED OUTPUTS
- A risk assessment report.
- Suggested "markup" or redlines for the document.
