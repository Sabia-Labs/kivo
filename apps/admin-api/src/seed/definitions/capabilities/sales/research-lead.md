# INSTRUCTIONS
Deeply research a potential lead or company to identify pain points and value alignment.

1. **Information Search:** Use available search tools to find the lead's current business challenges, recent news, and key decision-makers.
2. **Value Mapping:** Identify exactly how our product can solve their specific problems.
3. **Draft Insights:** Summarize why they are a good fit and what should be the "hook" for the first contact.
4. **CRM Update:** Document these insights in the project management tool or CRM.

# INPUTS
- Target Company or Lead Name.

# EXPECTED OUTPUTS
- A concise lead profile with identified "hooks" for outreach.
- Link to the updated record in the sales tool.
