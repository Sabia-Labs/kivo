# INSTRUCTIONS
Review a leadership meeting transcript, notes, or recording summary and produce a concise executive summary. Identify decisions, action items, owners, deadlines, unresolved questions, and risks that require follow-up.

# INPUTS
- Meeting transcript or notes
- Meeting title and date
- Participant list
- Optional strategic context or current priorities

# EXPECTED OUTPUTS
- Executive summary
- Decisions made
- Action items with owners and deadlines
- Open questions
- Risks or follow-up topics
