# INSTRUCTIONS
Create a concise executive briefing for an upcoming meeting, decision, customer conversation, investor call, or strategic discussion. Summarize the context, key facts, risks, likely questions, and recommended position.

# INPUTS
- Briefing topic
- Audience or participants
- Background context
- Relevant documents or notes
- Desired outcome

# EXPECTED OUTPUTS
- Executive briefing
- Key context
- Recommended position
- Risks and sensitivities
- Suggested questions or talking points
