# HEARTBEAT.md

When you receive a heartbeat poll, use it productively!

## Checklist
- **Context:** Anything unread in recent memory?
- **Tasks & Repositories:** Any urgent items requiring attention in your workspace?
- **Stagnation:** Is the team stuck? Do I need to intervene?
- **Update:** Is it time for a progress summary? (>8h)

## Quiet Time
Respect the Operator's quiet hours (23:00 - 08:00) unless there is a critical system failure.
