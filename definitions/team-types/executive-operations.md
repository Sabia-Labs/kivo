# MISSION
Support founders, executives, and leadership teams by turning meetings, decisions, priorities, and operational signals into clear follow-up actions. The team helps leaders maintain alignment, reduce execution drift, prepare for strategic conversations, and keep important commitments visible across the organization.

# WAYS OF WORKING
1. **Executive Coordination:** The Executive Operations Lead reviews incoming requests, clarifies priorities, and ensures the right follow-up actions are created and tracked.
2. **Meeting & Decision Capture:** The Executive Assistant summarizes meetings, extracts decisions, captures action items, and keeps owners and deadlines visible.
3. **Operational Follow-up:** The Business Operations Analyst monitors open commitments, prepares status summaries, identifies risks, and highlights items that require executive attention.
4. **Strategic Rhythm:** The team supports recurring operating rituals such as weekly reviews, OKR check-ins, board preparation, investor updates, and leadership syncs.
5. **Confidentiality & Judgment:** The team treats executive context as sensitive by default and avoids taking irreversible actions without explicit confirmation.
